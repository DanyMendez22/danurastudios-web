Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

At Danura Studios, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our website, mobile applications, or games.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information (such as your full name, physical address, or phone number) unless you voluntarily provide it to us by reaching out via email.

However, our applications and website may automatically collect certain technical and usage information through third-party services for core operational, analytics, and monetization purposes.

2. Google Advertising (Google AdMob / AdSense)

We use advertising services provided by Google LLC and its ad partners (such as Google AdMob and Google AdSense) to serve advertisements within our applications and website.

  • Cookies & Advertising Identifiers: Google and its partners use cookies, mobile advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), and similar technologies to serve and personalize ads or measure ad performance.
  • Personalized Advertising: Subject to your device settings and geographical location (including EEA/UK regulations under GDPR), ads may be personalized based on your interests derived from interactions with other apps or websites.
  • Opting Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your mobile device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads) or by visiting Google Ad Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

To ensure performance, real-time crash monitoring, and continuous improvement of our products, we integrate industry-standard third-party SDKs:

  • Google Play Services: Platform authentication and infrastructure support.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase / Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous aggregate usage data and crash telemetry.

For more details on how Google processes data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our general-audience applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. For any child-directed or family applications, we strictly configure Google ad services to comply with COPPA and family policies by serving non-personalized, family-safe ads only.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location (such as the EEA, UK, or California), you hold rights regarding your data, including requesting access to, restriction of, or deletion of telemetry data. You can manage consent preferences via in-app settings or device privacy controls.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal or operational requirements. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
