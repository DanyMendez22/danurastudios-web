Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Danura Studios, we respect and protect the privacy of users of our mobile applications, games, and websites. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications and services are designed to deliver the best experience with minimal necessary data collection:

  • Non-Personal Data: We may collect general technical information such as device type, operating system, and performance metrics to fix bugs and enhance our games and apps.
  • User-Provided Information: If you contact us directly (for instance, via customer support), we may retain your email address and message contents to assist you.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is strictly used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve our apps and games.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues.
  • Respond to user support inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services (such as analytics tools or standard advertising networks) that gather information according to their own privacy policies to help us understand general product usage.

4. Children's Privacy

Our products are developed in compliance with applicable child privacy protections. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental or legal guardian consent.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to us through our official website or support channels.
